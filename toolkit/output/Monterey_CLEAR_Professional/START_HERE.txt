COUNTY OF MONTEREY | DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL SERVICES
CLEAR Ownership: Staff Resource Toolkit — Professional Format Edition
Regenerated November 11, 2026 from the Monterey_CLEAR source package.

CONTENTS
18 editable Word documents and 18 matching PDFs, organized into six topic folders.
Each topic includes a one-page flyer, a one-page desk reference, and a two-page resource
(24 unique content pages). Word and PDF editions contain matching content.

1. CLEAR Communication Elements (CCEs)
2. Decision Escalation: full Ladder of Inference
3. Eisenhower Chart
4. Blank seven-task RACI Chart
5. Five Steps to a Respectful Reversal
6. Six Steps for a Clear Request, including escalation and follow-up

Monterey_CLEAR_Complete_Reading_Copy.pdf combines all 24 pages in order, with PDF
bookmarks for each topic and document type. It is flattened for preview and printing.

HOW TO USE
Flyer: post or share as a visual reminder (large type, one tool per page).
Desk reference: keep near the work for a quick check or reusable worksheet.
Resource: page 1 explains the tool; page 2 puts it to work with an example and a worksheet.

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS EDITION
- Every document was rebuilt in a single, consistent County of Monterey / DSS layout.
- Official artwork is now included: the County of Monterey seal and the Department of Social
  Services logo lockup ("Working Together for Our Community"), recovered from the approved
  Communicating CLEAR training deck, appear in every page header.
- Clearer visual hierarchy: numbered step cards, a stair-step Ladder of Inference, a color-coded
  Eisenhower grid, a four-tile RACI legend, shaded callouts, and lined worksheet areas.
- Page footers identify the series, topic, and document type, with "Page X of Y".
- Web sources (TED-Ed, USC Gould, FranklinCovey, Cornell IT) are live hyperlinks.
- Content, wording, sequence, and training citations are unchanged from the source edition.
  The approved Step 5 wording ("Use the correct route and verify timing") is preserved.

RACI CHARTS
All three RACI documents include seven blank task rows. Word tables are editable.
Each RACI PDF has 35 fillable text fields (Task, R, A, C, I for rows 1–7). Use a PDF reader
that supports form fields, then save a copy. The branch/team metadata lines can be completed
in Word or by hand. No County role assignments have been prefilled.

DESIGN AND BRANDING
Typeface: Open Sans (matches the approved CLEAR training series). The Word files reference
Open Sans; if it is not installed on a workstation, Word substitutes a similar sans-serif.
Palette: DSS logo navy (#1C3E5F), teal (#3D829A), and light blue tints, matching the County's
CLEAR Ownership staff fact sheet and training decks. Grey text (#5F6B73) for secondary copy.
Naming: "County of Monterey" is used for the organization throughout, per Board Policy G-140
(County of Monterey Branding Guidelines & Policies, June 2023), which also reserves the
organization name "Monterey County" for the geographic area only.

BEFORE RELEASE
Board Policy G-140 governs use of the County emblem and logo and requires County
Communications Director (or designee) approval of branded publications (Board Policy G-145).
Confirm the palette, logo clear space, and minimum size against the current G-140 PDF
(countyofmonterey.gov, Board Policy Manual) and obtain branding approval before distribution.

PRINTING
US Letter, portrait. Print at actual size / 100%. Each resource is exactly two pages and can be
printed double-sided. Each flyer and desk reference is one page.
