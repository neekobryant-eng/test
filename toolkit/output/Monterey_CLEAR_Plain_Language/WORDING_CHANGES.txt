CLEAR Ownership Staff Resource Toolkit — Plain-Language Edition
WORDING CHANGES

WHAT WAS CHANGED
- Titles now name the tool and the document type. Slogans like "Make direction clear."
  and "Change direction. Preserve trust." were replaced with titles such as
  "CLEAR Communication Elements (CCEs)" and "Five Steps for a Respectful Reversal".
- Section labels now say what the page is. "RESOURCE | UNDERSTAND THE TOOL" and
  "PUT IT TO WORK" became "RESOURCE" and "RESOURCE (CONTINUED)", with the topic named.
- Contrast phrasing ("Escalate a decision, not the whole problem", "Use by content, not by
  channel", "Change the decision, not the person's standing") was rewritten as direct statements.
- Formal words were replaced with everyday ones. Examples: "usable" became "clear";
  "intact" became "the same"; "verified location" became "where to find it";
  "route" became "process"; "decision boundary" became "limit"; "illustrative" became "made-up".
- Long sentences were split, and instructions now speak directly to the reader ("you").
- "Training basis:" became "Source:".

WHAT WAS NOT CHANGED
- The six CCE element labels, which match the Six-Element Message Template.
- The seven Ladder of Inference step names.
- The four Eisenhower quadrant labels: ACT NOW, SCHEDULE, CHECK & ROUTE, REDUCE OR STOP.
- The four RACI role names and their rules: at least one R and exactly one A per task.
- The five Respectful Reversal steps: Recognize, Explain, Own, Direct, Clarify.
- The six Clear Request step names, including the approved Step 5 wording,
  "Use the correct route and verify timing."
- The two-working-day response expectation and every urgent-situation instruction.
- Layout, artwork, page counts, and the 35 fillable fields in each RACI PDF.
