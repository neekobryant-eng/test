CLEAR Ownership Staff Resource Toolkit
COMPARING VERSION A AND VERSION B

Both versions use the same layout, County seal and DSS logo, page counts, and fillable
RACI fields. Only the wording is different. Pick one version per topic, or one for the
whole set.

Version A (folder: Monterey_CLEAR_Plain_Language)
  Plain, complete sentences. Titles name the tool, such as "How to Make a Clear Request".
  Reads like a handbook.

Version B (folder: Monterey_CLEAR_Plain_Language_Version_B)
  Shorter and more conversational, with contractions such as "don't" and "you'll".
  Titles follow a "Topic: what it does" pattern, such as "Clear Requests: Asking for a
  Decision". Reads like a coworker explaining it.

KEPT THE SAME IN BOTH
- The six CCE element labels, which match the Six-Element Message Template.
- The seven Ladder of Inference step names.
- The four Eisenhower quadrant labels: ACT NOW, SCHEDULE, CHECK & ROUTE, REDUCE OR STOP.
- The four RACI roles and their rules: at least one R and exactly one A per task.
- The five Respectful Reversal steps: Recognize, Explain, Own, Direct, Clarify.
- Clear Request steps 1-4.
- The two-working-day response standard and every urgent-situation instruction.

CLEAR REQUEST STEPS 5 AND 6
  Training wording   5: Use the correct route and verify timing
                     6: Track the reply and close the loop
  Version A          5: Confirm who decides and when
                     6: Track the reply and follow up
  Version B          5: Check who decides and the deadline
                     6: Follow up and share the outcome
  Note: the training slides and the Professional Format Edition still use the training
  wording. Align them, or note the difference, before release.

SAMPLE WORDING
  CCE flyer, element 2 ("Why / Why it is changing")
    A: Why is the work or the change needed?
    B: What is the reason?
  Eisenhower Q3 (CHECK & ROUTE)
    A: Confirm the request is real. Then route it, delegate it, or ask for more time, if
       you have the authority.
    B: Someone else's urgency. Confirm it, then pass it on, delegate it, or reset the
       timeline if you're allowed to.
  Respectful Reversal callout
    A: Don't blame the person. No one should be blamed for following the direction they
       were given.
    B: No blame. People who followed the direction they were given did the right thing.
